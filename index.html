Yannarak Wannasai

Vice President of IT Security at LINE MAN Wongnai. Application Security. Secure SDLC. Security Leadership.

CISSP · CCSP · CSSLP

Thai · English · 日本語

I am an innovative, results-driven, and analytical security leader with over 20 years of experience in the IT industry, focused on Application Security and the Secure Software Development Lifecycle (SSDLC).

I currently serve as Vice President of IT Security at LINE MAN Wongnai. Before that, I was Head of Security Engineering at Mercari, where I led a global organization of security managers and engineers covering product security, platform security, enterprise security, and AI security. Earlier in my career I led the application security team at Woven by Toyota, and held engineering and security roles at Rakuten, Kimberly-Clark, and Soft Square Group.

I hold a double Master of Science in Security and Mobile Computing from KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Aalto University, awarded through an Erasmus Mundus scholarship, and studied at Chulalongkorn University. I am a CISSP, CCSP, and CSSLP.

Outside of work, I have given back to the security community as co-leader of the OWASP Thailand Chapter, and helped Thai students pursue European higher education as the Erasmus Mundus Association’s country representative for Thailand.

My current interests include AI security, cloud security, identity and access management, and growing security engineering teams.

The best way to reach me is via LinkedIn or email.
